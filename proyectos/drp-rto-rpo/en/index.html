01The project in 60 seconds

Oracle recovers NetSuite. This plan secures everything else.

Oracle’s SLA (99.7% monthly) excludes scheduled maintenance, which can run around 15 h per quarter. If it counted against our 99.7%, it would use up the annual margin. That is why the target is measured as business service availability: NetSuite windows are covered by the BUs’ degraded mode.

IDEA 1

NetSuite is not failed over

Core DR belongs to Oracle by contract. The company enforces and verifies the SLA, and claims credit when it is missed.

IDEA 2

The BUs never stop

72 h local store-and-forward and their own folios: they sell, receive and invoice even when NetSuite does not respond.

IDEA 3

Zero lost messages

Active-active iPaaS in two regions, persistent queues, idempotent consumers and a DLQ reviewed daily.

IDEA 4

An own data copy

Incremental extraction ≤ 1 h and a daily immutable copy: lookups during an outage, ransomware defense and vendor exit.

RTO

Recovery Time Objective. Maximum time to restore the service.

RPO

Recovery Point Objective. Maximum data that can be lost.

MTPD

Max. Tolerable Period of Disruption. The point at which impact becomes unacceptable.

02SMART objectives and scope

Six measurable goals. "100% continuity" with an operational definition.

Zero transactions lost in integrations, and every BU able to run on its own for up to 72 h even when NetSuite is unavailable.

In scope (the company)

  • NetSuite integration via iPaaS (SuiteTalk, RESTlets)
  • IdP/SSO identity, DNS, WAN/SD-WAN for 9 BUs
  • Own copy of NetSuite data
  • CFDI 4.0 e-invoicing with PAC and host-to-host banking
  • Satellite applications and Oracle SLA management

Oracle’s responsibility

  • Infrastructure and database
  • NetSuite application
  • DR between data centers
  • 99.7% monthly SLA, excluding scheduled maintenance

Out of scope (corporate BCP)

  • Physical facilities continuity
  • Physical security
  • Human resources
  • Interface defined in the activation process

10 runbooks

10Testing and exercise plan

The test that validates the plan: disconnecting NetSuite for all 9 BUs.

NetSuite cannot be failed over, so what gets tested is the continuity around it. The program scales from tabletop exercises to the full test once a year.

The cycle of every test

Plan approved by the CAB with scope, window and rollback criteria.
Execution with a timekeeper and a time log per step.
Report within 5 business days: actual vs. target RTO/RPO, findings and owners.
Runbook updates and follow-up in the Continuity Committee.
11Crisis communication

If the IdP goes down, so does email. Channels don’t depend on the ERP.

One spokesperson per audience and updates at committed times. Printed directory, also kept in an offline vault, reviewed every quarter.
