02Project Charter

Six SMART goals. Measurable from day one.

The charter authorizes the Head of IT as Program Manager within $22.0 MM MXN and 24 months. Any deviation above 5% in cost or 4 weeks on the critical path escalates to the Steering Committee.

In scope

Tap a module to see what it covers.

Out of scope

  • Payroll and core HR: integrated, not replaced.
  • Transaction history > 2 years: archived in a read-only data lake.
  • Custom development that duplicates standard functionality. Rule: adopt before you adapt.

Key assumptions

  • Microsoft SaaS (SLA 99.9%); the remaining 0.2% is the margin for network, integrations and own operations.
  • Certified partner, Success by Design methodology, fixed price per deliverable.
  • Key users at 50% from M3 to M15, with budgeted backfill.
  • Change freeze on legacy processes from M12.
